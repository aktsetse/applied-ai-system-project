Mathematics is the study of numbers, patterns, and relationships.
Even numbers are divisible by 2 without a remainder.
Odd numbers always leave a remainder of 1 when divided by 2.
A prime number has exactly two positive divisors: 1 and itself.
A composite number has more than two divisors.
A perfect square is an integer that is the square of another integer.
A factorial is the product of all positive integers up to a given number.
The decimal system is base 10 because humans have ten fingers.
Zero is the only integer that is neither positive nor negative.
The Fibonacci sequence begins 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21.
